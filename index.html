Heritage

Club Story & Founding Timeline

Born from passion for fast-paced football and tight-knit camaraderie, JJSC FC grew from kickabouts and local fixtures into a structured club with its own crest, rival storylines, and matchday culture. Every whistle is a chance to defend the badge.

“Play Fast, Stand Together, Leave Everything on the Pitch.”

Founding Spark
First squad sessions and core group formation under the JJSC FC name.
Rivalry Era
Heated fixtures ignite against Royal FC, JhonJhonia FC, Adamjee Blackouts, and 3 Bros.
12-Man Core
Structured squad roles, formation boards, and stat tracking take center stage.
2026 Digital Hub
