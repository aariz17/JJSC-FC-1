      The Jhonjhonia Story

      FROM LOCAL TURF TO CHAMPIONSHIP PEDIGREE

      Founded by passionate youth footballers in Jhonjhonia & Gopalganj, Jhonjhonia Jubo Sporting Club (JJSC FC) united twelve core brothers under President Meherun Nesa, Captain Aariz (#17), and Manager Hamza (#47).

      Known for rapid one-touch passing, fearless pressing, and royal-navy-and-gold pride, JJSC FC dominates local 11v11, 7v7, 6v6, and 4v4 Victory League arenas.

      Home Turf: Jhonjhonia Arena Colors: Navy • Pitch Green • Gold
      Chapter 01 • Foundation

      THE JHONJHONIA PACT

      Aariz (#17), Hamza (#47), and Nafiz (#7) assemble the original 12-player brotherhood and adopt the JhonJhonia FC rising-sun shield crest.

      Chapter 02 • Derby Dominance

      CONQUERING LOCAL RIVALS

      Statement derby triumphs against Royal FC, JhonJhonia FC, Adamjee Blackouts, and 3 Bros cement JJSC FC as the region's premier club.

      Chapter 03 • 500+ G+A Era

      CENTURY MILESTONES

      Captain Aariz surpasses 90+ assists while Manager Hamza crosses 65+ career goals, driving the club past 530 combined goal contributions.

      Chapter 04 • 2026 Digital Arena

      VICTORY LEAGUE & 2026 KIT

      Launch of the official Royal Navy calligraphic Home Kit (৳520), custom club anthem, and 3D tactical command portal.
